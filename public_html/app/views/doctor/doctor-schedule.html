Important Information & Tips
Schedule Guidelines:
  • Create schedules for specific dates when the doctor is available
  • Each schedule is unique to a particular date
  • Slot duration should be between 10-60 minutes
  • Break time is optional but recommended for lunch/rest periods
  • Inactive schedules won't generate appointment slots
Quick Actions:
  • Edit: Modify timings and settings for a date
  • Duplicate: Copy schedule to create for another date
  • Toggle: Temporarily enable/disable schedule without deleting
  • Delete: Permanently remove schedule
  • Bulk Create: Quickly create schedules for next 7 days
Note: Slots are generated based on these date-specific schedules. When booking appointments, only dates with active schedules will show available slots.
